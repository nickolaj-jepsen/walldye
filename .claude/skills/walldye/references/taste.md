# The owner's taste

The family look is a quiet ground, a few grey steps between bg and fg, and one restrained
accent event drawn from the accent ramp. Minimal, with plenty of empty space.

The owner reviewed 153 designs on 2026-09-27 and rejected 32. The liked names below are pieces
in `wallpapers/<slug>/`; the rejected designs were not kept, so only their names are left here.

Liked:

- dithered pieces, with a request for more dither strategies and more pixel art;
- instrument, science and developer pieces: radar-sweep, helicorder, navball, smith-chart,
  schematic, git-graph, minimap;
- quiet textures holding a small accent event;
- crisp patent and technical drawings of one object: patent-lamp, engine-section, airfoil,
  ray-diagram, watch-movement.

Rejected:

- big, flat, saturated accent shapes: tomoe, vertigo-spiral, vega-bulge, red-fuji, lava-lamp;
- clip-art illustration: winter-tree, fireflies, iceberg, rain-moon;
- maximalist low-poly landscapes;
- busy full-sheet blueprints: orthographic, floorplan, exploded-bearing.

In practice: colour only with tokens, keep accent areas small, and lean toward dither, pixel,
technical and instrument subjects.

Variants are curated, not generated. A piece may carry up to four named variants, and only
when each one changes what is depicted: a moon phase, a rule number, a reaction regime, the
moment of a sweep. A nudged value or a new seed is not a variant. Agents propose at most three,
always as drafts, and the owner approves each one in review. Breadth still comes first: a new
subject beats another version of an old one.
